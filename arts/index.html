A little more clarity.

How do I enquire about a portrait?

Use the enquiry button on the work you want. The newer portrait originals are £5,000 each and Inner Child is £25,000, all as artist-set asking prices. The artist will confirm availability, dimensions, material, condition and delivery before any purchase agreement or payment.

What format is each artwork?

The two portrait listings are for physical originals, shown using digitally polished canvas-style previews. Inner Child is supplied as a digital composition and offered as a single-piece edition. The final physical format, dimensions and any rights included must be agreed before purchase. The listings do not claim that a physical canvas already exists where this has not been confirmed.

How many physical originals exist?

The artist states that there are two physical originals of each portrait design: four originals across the two designs in total. The newer original of each design is the piece listed here, described as 2 of 2. This is the artist’s declared limitation, not an independent authentication or a claim that digital copies of the images cannot exist.

Why are the earlier originals £10,000 and these £5,000?

The artist has set an asking price of £10,000 for the first original of each design, and £5,000 for the newer original of each design offered here. These are artist-set asking prices, not independent valuations, recorded sale prices or guarantees of resale value. The earlier originals are not included in either £5,000 listing.

Is Inner Child limited to one piece?

Yes. The artist is offering Inner Child as a single-piece edition, numbered 1 of 1. It is separate from the two portrait designs and their four-original total. The edition refers to the offered work; it does not imply that copies of its online image cannot exist. Final format and any exclusivity or reproduction rights must be stated in the purchase agreement.

Can I ask about a commission?

You can send a commission enquiry. We will discuss the subject, size, timescale and availability before agreeing a brief; no booking is confirmed through this website.
