A simple explanation of this website, its public demos and the ways you can contact BonusPips.

Who this website represents

BonusPips is the umbrella brand connecting Dhmx Arts, Ownly, Tasky Skill Proof, BonusPips Trading and the proposed BonusPips Digital venture. A project or brand name does not by itself mean a separately incorporated company.

Website contact: Dhmx.co@gmail.com.

Browsing

This website does not add advertising or analytics scripts and does not set advertising or analytics cookies. Pages and assets are hosted through GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process ordinary technical information, including IP addresses and request logs, to operate and secure the service. Read GitHub’s privacy information for its own processing.

Enquiries

Contact buttons and enquiry forms open your email application. You choose whether to send the draft. Form contents are not submitted to a database or mailing list by this website. An email you send is handled through your email provider and our Gmail inbox, so include only information needed for the enquiry. Contact us about access to or deletion of correspondence.

Public product demos

The Ownly and Tasky demos work in your browser. Ownly can import an exported JSON backup locally; the file is checked before you confirm a merge. Tasky can save recipient capsules into a local skills hub. Neither feature sends the records to us. Entries remain in the current session unless you explicitly use a local-save button. Saved records are stored in localStorage under this website’s origin, without encryption; they can be accessed by someone with access to the same browser profile and are not synced to other devices.

Use non-sensitive examples. Do not enter private client, employer, financial, identity or confidential evidence information. The demo save and export actions do not upload your records to us. Exports create a local JSON download; sharing that file is a separate action you control.

Use the demo’s clear button to remove its saved records or clear this site’s storage in your browser settings. Clearing browser storage can permanently remove saved entries. Export anything you need first.

External links

Instagram, linked receipt or evidence URLs, and any restricted product previews are separate services. Their own access controls and privacy practices apply. Links entered in demos are not automatically fetched by those demos.

Art and service enquiries

Art listings display artist-set asking prices and digitally polished presentation images. The source photograph is available for reference. No artwork checkout or payment processing is available on this website. Availability, material, size, condition, delivery and purchase terms must be agreed before a sale. BonusPips Digital is a proposed venture and has no active booking or payment flow.

Demo limitations

Ownly’s dates are organisational estimates, not a confirmation of warranty coverage or a legal assessment. Tasky capsules are self-reported evidence records; fingerprints are integrity indicators, not independent proof of competence or authorship.

Updates

This information will be updated when the website adds new data processing, payment services or public product features.
