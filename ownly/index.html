When something goes wrong.

A useful starting checklist for purchase admin:

  • Find your purchase date, receipt and seller details.
  • Write down the fault and when you noticed it.
  • Check the seller’s process and any warranty terms.
  • Keep copies of messages and the outcome you request.

This demo does not decide legal rights, submit claims or contact a seller.

A little more clarity.

Is this the complete Ownly product?

No. This public demo illustrates the ownership vault. The fuller prototype also explores a post-purchase Action Desk, and remains available only to approved testers.

Where is my information stored?

Until you choose “Save in this browser”, entries stay only in this page session. Optional saving uses this browser’s local storage, without encryption or cross-device sync. The demo does not upload your entries.

Can Ownly send a claim or guarantee a warranty?

No. This demo helps organise information and dates. It does not provide a legal determination, guarantee manufacturer coverage or contact anyone for you.
