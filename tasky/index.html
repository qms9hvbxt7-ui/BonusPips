A little more clarity.

Does a capsule mean my skills are verified?

No. The current demo records self-reported claims and user-attached evidence. Independent provenance checks, contributor attestations and challenge verification are not implemented.

What does the fingerprint mean?

It is a SHA-256 digest of the exact recipient evidence object. Matching fingerprints indicate matching content, not authorship, ownership or competence. A recipient must still assess the evidence.

Does exporting publish my information?

No. Exporting downloads a JSON file to your device. Sharing that file with someone is your own separate action. Optional fields are excluded unless selected.
