Ownly

Working prototype · Private preview

Your possessions come with receipts, dates and decisions. Ownly brings those details together in an ownership vault and an Action Desk for purchase problems.

  • Purchase & warranty records
  • Receipt links & notes
  • Guided next steps
  • Repair or replace comparisons
Open private previewRequest early access

Preview access is restricted to approved users. The prototype saves records in the current browser; cloud sync and automatic claim submission are not available.
