About BonusPips-fx

BonusPips-fx is a trading and market education brand focused on clear execution, consistency, and disciplined risk management. The aim is to present market structure in a simple, people-friendly way—so you can follow the logic behind each idea and learn the process.

Simple Explanations

We break down market structure and trade ideas in a clear, easy-to-understand way. Instead of overcomplicating charts, we focus on why price is moving, what matters, and where risk is defined, so both beginners and experienced traders can follow the logic behind each idea.

Risk-first approach

Every trade idea starts with risk management. We prioritise clear invalidation levels, sensible position sizing, and disciplined execution. The goal is not to chase wins, but to protect capital and maintain consistency over time.
